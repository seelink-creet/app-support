# Colorist Eye Privacy Policy

Updated September 30, 2026

Colorist Eye is developed and provided by LinkLiu. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact support.

## 1. In-app data processing

Colorist Eye requires no account. It currently includes no third-party ads, usage analytics or cross-app tracking.

Photo processing, practice-image generation and scope calculations take place on your device. The app does not proactively upload imported photos, practice records or settings to a developer server. The developer cannot remotely read them through the app.

## 2. Photos and practice copies

When importing photos, you choose them through the system photo picker. The app receives only the images you select and does not scan your entire library.

The app stores processed practice copies in its own storage for later exercises. Importing and practicing do not change the original photos in your system library.

You can remove a local practice image with “Delete copy” in the app library. This does not delete the original from your photo library.

## 3. Practice records and settings

The app stores practice records, daily-challenge results and settings such as the sound switch on your device to show results and remember preferences.

The app does not proactively send this information to the developer or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 4. Retention, deletion and backups

Imported practice copies remain in the app until you delete them or remove the app and its local data.

Deleting the app removes its local data from the device. The system's “Offload App” feature may retain documents and data.

Depending on device settings, app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. Backups are managed by Apple and your chosen backup method; the developer cannot access your device backups. Deleting local data does not immediately remove corresponding data from existing backups. Manage backups in system settings.

## 5. Support email

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you include. These are used only to handle feedback, investigate issues and reply.

Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary sensitive information. Support email is kept only as needed to handle the issue, follow up or meet applicable legal requirements, and is not used for advertising. You may request deletion through the email address below, except where retention is required by law.

The email provider processes mail transmission and storage under its own terms and privacy policy.

## 6. Support site and external services

This privacy policy and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, the hosting service may process necessary access logs, such as IP addresses. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Visiting the website is separate from local photo processing in the app. Opening the support page does not upload your practice photos or records to the site.

## 7. Policy updates

If app features or data practices change, this policy and the update date above will be revised. Where separate notice or consent is required, it will be handled under applicable requirements.
