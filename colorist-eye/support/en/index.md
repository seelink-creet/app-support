# Colorist Eye Support

Colorist Eye is a visual training app for spotting color casts, restoring RGB balance, and reading scopes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a professionally calibrated display?

The exercises focus on comparing images on the same screen. Display settings and ambient light affect what you see; this app does not replace professional display calibration.

### Can I practice with my own photos?

Yes. After a one-time Pro purchase, you can select photos with the system photo picker and import them for practice.

### Does deleting a practice copy remove the original photo?

No. “Delete copy” removes only the practice image stored in the app. It does not change the original in your photo library.
