# One-Button Swordsman Support

Contact: [seelink@sina.com](mailto:seelink@sina.com)

For help, include your device model, iOS version, game version, display language, and what happened. Screenshots can help. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

## Language
Choose your display language in the game's settings.

## Sound
Check the in-game audio settings, device volume, and connected headphones or Bluetooth output.

## Saved progress
Progress is stored locally. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting a saved journey. The game has no account-based or cloud-save recovery service. Device backups are managed separately by your device.

## Purchases and refunds
Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. For refund requests, use [Apple's refund service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).
