# One-Button Swordsman Privacy Policy

Updated October 3, 2026

## Game data
One-Button Swordsman is a single-player game and does not require an account. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. The game's save system does not upload progress to a developer server, and no game-account or cloud-save service is provided.

The game reads your device's language and region settings to choose its initial display language. This does not use GPS or determine your physical location. You can change the display language in the game.

Game artwork and audio are included in the app. The game does not integrate third-party advertising, usage analytics, cross-app tracking, or in-app purchases. Your App Store purchase is handled by Apple; the game does not receive your payment-card details.

## Your device and Apple services
Deleting the app removes its local app data. Backups managed by your device may retain data according to your backup settings. Apple may process purchases, backups or diagnostics under its own policies and your device settings. These are separate from the game's local save system.

## Contacting support
If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are received through the support mailbox so that your request can be handled. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Contact the same address if you have questions about the information you supplied or wish to request its deletion. Do not delete the game while seeking help with local progress unless you intend to remove its local data.

## This website
This policy and the support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). These pages do not add analytics or advertising scripts.
